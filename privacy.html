Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 · Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to Shikshapatri Companion ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and providing a clean, respectful, advertisement-free spiritual environment. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application across iOS, iPadOS, and Android platforms.

1. Offline-First Philosophy

The Shikshapatri Companion app is engineered with an offline-first design principle. All 212 Sanskrit verses, English transliterations, Gujarati translations, Hindi commentaries, and chapter categorizations are stored locally on your device within an embedded SQLite database.

You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to read the sacred scriptures, study commentary, or use the audio pronunciation player.

2. Information We Collect (Optional Cloud Features)

If you voluntarily choose to sign in to enable cross-device cloud synchronization or streak tracking, we may collect the following minimal data points:

  • Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or Email authentication, we receive your email address, unique account identifier (UID), and display name provided by the respective authentication provider.
  • Spiritual Reading Journal & Progress: If signed in, we synchronize your list of completed shlokas, bookmarked verse IDs, custom study notes, and reading streak count to secure cloud storage.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Anonymous Analytics: We collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as app crash logs, device model, operating system version, and general feature engagement events) via Google Firebase Analytics to fix software bugs and improve app reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and restore your spiritual reading progress across multiple devices.
  • To maintain and improve the technical stability, speed, and reliability of the App.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues (such as platform-specific sign-in failures).
  • To respond to your support requests or technical inquiries when you contact us.

4. Zero Advertising & No Data Sale

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties. Our app is entirely free from commercial banner advertisements, pop-ups, tracking pixels, and data brokers.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

The App utilizes reputable third-party infrastructure solely to deliver cloud functionality:

  • Google Firebase: Used for Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (progress synchronization), and Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics. Firebase operates under Google's enterprise security protocols.
  • Apple Sign-In: Used on iOS and iPadOS devices to provide private, secure authentication with optional email masking.
  • Google Sign-In: Used on Android and iOS devices to enable convenient one-tap login.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion Rights

We retain your synchronized reading progress only as long as your account remains active. You maintain complete ownership of your data and possess the right to delete it at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly within the app by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action immediately purges your profile and reading records from Cloud Firestore.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You may also email our privacy team at shikshapatari@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will verify and completely purge your account data within 48 hours.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Shikshapatri is a sacred scripture suitable for readers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such data.

8. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS) for all data transmitted between the App and cloud servers. Stored cloud records are encrypted at rest using Google Cloud enterprise security.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our volunteer development team at:
