Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries & Guides

Clear, straightforward solutions for all app features and platform setup.

You can read all 212 shlokas completely offline without signing in. To back up your bookmarks, reading progress, and completion streaks across multiple devices, sign in using Google, Apple ID, or your Email under Settings > Account. Your progress is automatically synchronized in the background whenever your device connects to the internet.

Yes! All 212 shlokas in Sanskrit Devanagari, English transliteration, Gujarati translation, Hindi commentary, and English meanings are bundled directly into the app using a local SQLite database. You do not need any internet connection to read, bookmark, take notes, or listen to audio chanting.

When you sign in using Google, Apple ID, or Email, your reading milestones, completed shlokas, and custom bookmarks are backed up to secure Cloud Firestore. When you switch to a new phone or tablet, simply sign in with the same account, and your entire spiritual journal is restored automatically.

Navigate to Settings > Voice & Pronunciation. You can choose from built-in voice presets (Calm Meditation, Traditional Chanting, Study Pace) or customize the speech rate, volume, and pitch to your personal preference.

While viewing any shloka, tap the Share button at the top right of the card. A sacred poster preview will generate with gold filigree borders. Tap Share Poster to open your phone's native share menu, where you can select Instagram Stories, WhatsApp, Facebook, or Save to Photos. You can also tap Copy to copy the Sanskrit and translation text directly.

You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from within the app by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account. You can also email us at shikshapatari@gmail.com with your registered email ID, and our team will permanently purge your data within 48 hours.
