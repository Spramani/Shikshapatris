On Android, Google Sign-In relies on Google Play Services verifying your app's digital certificate fingerprint against the Firebase Console. If you see ApiException: 10 (DEVELOPER_ERROR) or sign-in failure, it indicates that your Android build's SHA-1 fingerprint has not yet been registered in Firebase.

Steps to resolve in Firebase Console:

  1. Go to Firebase Console > select your project shikshapatri-e8187.
  2. Click on Project Settings (gear icon) > scroll down to Your apps > select the Android app with package name com.shikshapatri.App.
  3. Click Add fingerprint and paste your SHA-1 key:
    BF:37:35:D6:5C:91:6C:3D:1E:51:93:07:3B:64:1B:CD:AE:94:ED:57
  4. Also add the SHA-256 fingerprint:
    67:23:80:C5:A7:3B:82:A9:F6:F5:AD:DE:04:BC:0C:60:53:7D:24:2C:BC:74:54:07:2D:0D:CE:88:7D:5E:FE:E5
  5. Download the updated google-services.json file and place it in android/app/google-services.json.
  6. Clean and rebuild: flutter clean && flutter run. Google Sign-In will now authenticate seamlessly!
